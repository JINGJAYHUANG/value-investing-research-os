# 05 — Research Status Model

**Status:** FINAL for framework governance

## Valid states

### QUEUED
Research topic identified; no reliable module should be treated as complete.

### DRAFT
Initial synthesis exists, but sourcing, counter-evidence, or coverage remains incomplete.

### REVIEWED
Sources and reasoning have been checked against the module's review checklist. Remaining limitations are documented.

### FINAL
The module meets its declared scope and quality gate. `FINAL` never means eternal truth; it means the current version passed the defined gate.

### RETIRED
Superseded, invalidated, or intentionally removed from active use.

## Promotion gate: DRAFT → REVIEWED

Require:

- explicit scope;
- primary-source coverage where possible;
- citations/source ledger;
- no unresolved major attribution errors;
- counter-evidence section;
- known limitations;
- date/version stamp.

## Promotion gate: REVIEWED → FINAL

Require:

- independent re-check of material claims;
- clear distinction between source statement and project interpretation;
- no copyrighted source reproduction beyond lawful quotation limits;
- smoke tests pass;
- change log entry;
- declared coverage boundary.

## Anti-overclaim rule

A `QUEUED`, `DRAFT`, or `REVIEWED` named-investor module may not be described as “the finalized X method,” “X's complete brain,” or equivalent language.
